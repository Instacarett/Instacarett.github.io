Why

Getting medical advice shouldn't take days especially when we have so many talented medical professionals who struggle to find jobs in Trinidad and Tobago.

Approach

People often struggle to leave their beds or houses to get the care they need due to ailment, transportation or transportation costs. Most of these persons have access to the internet, so why bring the patient to the doctor when they can both be connected, instantly (and from anywhere).
