Answers about instacare and the experience we’re building.

What is instacare?

instacare is a medical app being built to connect people with experienced doctors and emerging medical talent for medical advice on their device.

Does instacare cover physical and mental health?

Yes. Our planned services bring physical health and mental wellbeing into one care journey. Service availability will be confirmed at launch.

How will it work?

Sign up or log in, tell us what you’re feeling, connect with a doctor by video call and receive a follow-up within 48 hours.

Is the app available now?

The app is coming soon. Follow @instacarett on Instagram for launch updates.

Where can I find pricing?

Pricing and payment details will be shared before launch.

Can I join as a doctor?

Visit our Sign up as a doctor page to learn about the planned sign-up process and express your interest.
