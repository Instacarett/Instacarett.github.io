How it works

Feeling better starts
with a conversation.

From your first question to a follow-up with your doctor, here’s how instacare brings medical advice closer to you.

Explore the steps
01Sign up / Login

STEP 01 · Sign up / Login

Your first step to feeling better.

Access your instacare account to begin your journey to feeling better.

Create or sign in to your account from your device, then get ready to share what’s on your mind.

02Tell us what you’re feeling

STEP 02 · Tell us what you’re feeling

Start with your story.

Fill out the details of exactly what you’re feeling so that we can match you with the best possible doctor.

Physical health or mental wellbeing: sharing your concerns helps guide the conversation.

03Connect with a doctor

STEP 03 · Connect with a doctor

A real conversation. On your device.

Consult directly through a video call and receive the advice you need to feel better.

Talk through your concerns, ask questions and understand your next steps.

04Stay supported

STEP 04 · Stay supported

Care continues after the call.

A follow-up from your doctor within 48 hours helps you stay on track.

Keep your questions together and use the follow-up to discuss how you’re getting on.
